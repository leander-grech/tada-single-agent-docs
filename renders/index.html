    Render gallery¶

    Every video on this site, credited to the model that flew it. A caption bar names the model, the checkpoint, the scenario seed and how the episode was chosen (deterministic policy, best of 10 sampled attempts, or lookahead); hover a video for its metadata file. Videos whose model cannot be established with certainty are kept under Archive.

    Each frame: the map on the left, and on the right every flight's predicted do-nothing descent (altitude against time from now); ▼ is the predicted touchdown, the tick on the ground line the AMAN target, and solid red a predicted loss of separation.
