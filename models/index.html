    Leaderboard¶

    Every number on this page is computed from the evaluation files listed on each model's card. Click a column to sort; the champion row is highlighted.

    Champion rule (windowed): only models that lose separation on at most 5 of the 100 validation seeds qualify (20-flight, deterministic); among those, most seeds solved, then fewer separation losses, then more hard-solved, then fewer clearances. The rule deliberately puts safety first: a model with more solves but more losses of separation does not take the title.
