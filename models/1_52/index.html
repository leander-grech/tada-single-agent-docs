    1_52¶

    complete release MDP point merge

    What changed: 1_50 fine-tuned on the BGY point merge (use case 2), release MDP as 1_50, stitched 2×20 point-merge streams from a pre-screened pool of feasible ones. LR 1e-4 to 1e-5, 100k critic warm-up, seed 52.

    Release MDP

    This model trained in the release MDP, unlike the champion and every model before 1_49: after each step the front of the landing queue is released once a flight is locked (predicted within 30 s of its target and in no predicted conflict). A released flight leaves the window and can no longer be cleared. It is scored in the same environment, on the same seeds and with the same metrics, so its numbers measure landings in the same scenarios, but under that extra constraint on the agent.

    Point merge

    Trained and scored on the BGY point merge (VALIDATION_USE_CASE_2), not the MXP trombone: every score on this card is a point-merge score, on the same 100 validation seeds. It is compared only with point-merge scores: its parent's and the MXP champion's zero-shot scores (Point merge), or a point-merge parent's own.

    About this run

    Trained after the bust clawback (code commit 618a073): a loss of separation takes back the release payments of flights still airborne. Its training pool excludes the validation candidates.
