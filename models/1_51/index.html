    1_51¶

    in progress release MDP

    What changed: 1_49 again, with feasible-only training from a pre-screened pool: feasible stitched 2×20 streams screened up front (validation and held-out streams removed) instead of redrawn at reset. Release MDP: locked flights released from the window (±30 s), bracket paid at release, flat deviation potential. 1_48 fine-tuned, LR 3e-5 to 1e-5, 300k critic warm-up, seed 49.

    Release MDP

    This model trained in a different MDP from every earlier model, the champion included: after each step the front of the landing queue is released once a flight is locked (predicted within 30 s of its target and in no predicted conflict). A released flight leaves the window and can no longer be cleared. It is scored in the same environment, on the same seeds and with the same metrics, so its numbers measure landings in the same scenarios, but under that extra constraint on the agent.

    About this run

    Trained with a reward flaw since fixed (code commit 618a073): with pay at release, a flight's bracket paid at release was kept even when the episode then ended in a loss of separation, so a bust cost less than the objective says. Runs after that commit take the release payments of flights still airborne back on a bust. This is a plausible contributor to any excess of losses, not a proven one.
